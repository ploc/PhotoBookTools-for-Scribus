# PhotoBookTools-for-Scribus

‘PhotoBook Tools’ is a collection of scripts and tricks intended to create photo album pages in a fast and flexible way with Scribus. Please read the ‘Instructions’-file for installation and use!

Summary of workflow:
1) ‘PhotoBookLayoutMaker’-script generates image frame layouts (you can save them in the Scrapbook for future use).
2) Insert images in bulk into your image frames.
3) ‘PhotoBookFillFramesCentered’-script performs automatically a maximal fill of the selected image frames. If needed you can manually adjust.
4) ‘PhotoBookImageCropResize’-script will crop your images to the image frames and resize them to the desired dpi (reduction of file size).
5) Edit caption texts (if you have created them in step 1).
6) Export your photo book to pdf or other formats.

Alternative for steps 1 to 3: the ‘PhotoBookWebGUI’-script opens a web page in your browser to build the whole book: choose the page size (when no document is open), pick the images of the book on your drive, add pages or double pages, then select images and click one of the proposed layouts for the page or double page. Frames are created in the Scribus document with the images filled and centered; captions and text blocks can then be written in Scribus. In the page you can also crop and zoom an image in its frame, rotate it by quarter turns (JPEG images get a copy with another EXIF orientation, without recompression, in a ‘PhotoBook rotated’ folder next to them) or mirror it, drag images onto frames or between frames, insert double pages between existing ones, reorder double pages by dragging them, choose layouts with one image across the fold of a double page, and undo the last actions. Once the book has a file (button ‘Save’ the first time), every change is saved automatically, and undo stays possible until the page is closed. The images of the book can be shown all together, by folder or by day (the day the photo was taken, from its EXIF information), with folders and days that can be folded; a small label tells the quality of each image for your page size (HD: good for a whole page, OK: up to half a page, Low: small frames only), and frames whose image has too few pixels (under 150 dpi) are marked in the page. Click a caption or a text block to write its text. The paragraph styles ‘PhotoBookCaption’ and ‘PhotoBookText’ are created once: change them in Scribus (Edit > Styles) to change all the captions or all the texts of the book. If a text was formatted in Scribus (fonts, sizes, colors), it is outlined in orange and the page warns that editing it there would lose this formatting. The borders of a selected frame can be moved with the handles in their middle, and the frame itself, across or up and down, with the handle in its center (✥); frames stay inside the margins and do not cover each other: the border sticks to the margins, to the borders of the other frames and at the gap of the layout from them; an image keeps its zoom and center, its caption stays under it, and the page is then marked as adjusted by hand (changing the options does not lay it out again, choosing a layout does). To edit a page made with the script in an earlier session, just click it: its photos are shown as the selected images, as when it was made, and other layouts are proposed for them (images can be added or removed in the left panel), the options of the page (captions, text block, spacing…) are shown, and the photos keep their crop, rotation and mirror, each caption follows its photo. When captions or text blocks are removed, their texts are kept aside with the page (the page shows them) and come back when captions or a text block are added again. Changing the options (captions, text block, spacing…) updates the current layout of the page. HEIC images are converted to JPEG (on macOS, or with Pillow installed). Keep ‘PhotoBookWebGUI.html’, ‘PhotoBookWebGUI.cfg’ and ‘PhotoBookLanguage.py’ in the same folder as the script. Scribus does not respond while the web page is open: press ‘Close’ in the page to come back to Scribus (the book is then saved, and Scribus may take a few seconds to redraw the pages).

Easier access on macOS: double-click ‘Créer l'icône Livre photo.command’ (in the PhotoBookTools folder) to create the application ‘Livre photo’ in your Applications folder, and drag it to the Dock. Opening it starts Scribus with the script; dropping a Scribus book (.sla) on it opens this book with the script. If Scribus is already open, use Script > Recent Scripts in Scribus (after the first time with Script > Execute Script…).

Emojis as stickers (PhotoBookWebGUI):
Scribus cannot print color emojis in texts (emojis typed in a caption disappear from the printed book). Use instead the ‘Sticker’ button above the page: click an emoji of the list, or type or paste any other emoji in the ‘Other emoji’ field (emoji picker: Ctrl+Cmd+Space on Mac, Windows+. on Windows), and it is placed on the page as a color image (PNG with a transparent background, 1024 pixels), that you can drag anywhere on the page or double page, resize or delete. The images are saved in a ‘PhotoBook stickers’ folder next to the book (or next to its images while the book is not saved yet): keep this folder with the book. When the computer is connected to the internet, the stickers are drawn from Twemoji (https://github.com/jdecked/twemoji), graphics licensed under CC-BY 4.0: credit them in your book, for example ‘Emojis: Twemoji, © Twitter and other contributors, CC-BY 4.0’. Without internet, the emojis of your system are used (Apple, Microsoft or Google emojis, which are less sharp when printed large, and whose use is ruled by their owners).

For Scribus 1.5.6 and higher (needs Python 3; tested in Windows 10 and Linux).
